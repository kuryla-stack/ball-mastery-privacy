# Privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-09-26

Ball Mastery: Skills Academy is an offline football training application. This policy explains what the app stores and what it does not collect.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores training state locally on the device using the platform preferences store. This state can include onboarding completion, theme choice, cue preferences, session length, completed skill IDs, academy XP, streak count, challenge scores, and the local session history.

The session history contains the skill name, repetitions, XP, session duration, and the date and time recorded by the device. It is used only to display your progress inside the app.

## Information not collected

The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. It does not request your name, contact details, account credentials, precise location, contacts, photos, files, health information, or payment details. It has no advertising identifier, analytics, crash-reporting service, account system, cloud sync, or remote content service.

The app does not use a network connection and is designed to remain usable in airplane mode.

## Your choices

You can change theme, sound cues, haptics, and session length in Settings. You can delete individual session-history entries. The Reset academy data action removes the app's stored training state from the device and returns the app to onboarding. Uninstalling the app also removes its local application data according to the device's normal storage behavior.

## Children and adults

The product is intended for an adult audience and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. No account or age information is requested inside the app.
